PRO TIP FOR STUDENTS

How to Play Any Video from Apna College or Course Websites

Apna College, Physics Wallah, Coursera, Udemy aur lagbhag har educational website videos ko HLS (.m3u8) ya direct stream format me deliver karti hai. Is player me unhe chalana 100% possible aur bahut aasan hai!

1

Apna College / Website par lecture kholo

Apne browser (Chrome, Brave, Edge, etc.) me course lecture page open karo aur video ko 2 second ke liye play karo.

2

Inspect DevTools kholo (F12)

Keyboard par F12 (Mac par Cmd + Option + I) dabao aur "Network" tab par click karo.

3

Filter box me likho: m3u8 ya mp4

Network tab ke search/filter box me m3u8 type karo. Aapko master.m3u8 ya index.m3u8 jaisi file dikhegi.

4

Right click karke "Copy URL" karo

Us file par Right Click karo → Copy → Copy link address (ya Copy URL).

5

OmniPlayer me paste karo aur Play karo!

OmniPlayer ke search bar me link paste karo. Player turant HLS.js engine use karke ultra-smooth 1080p stream shuru kar dega, sath me 300% Audio Booster aur Notes bhi milenge!

Tip: Agar YouTube video hai toh direct YouTube link daal do, agar local downloaded file hai toh "Upload File" dabao!
